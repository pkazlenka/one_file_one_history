# One file - One history

In this opensource collection of short novels I'm trying to  tell random stories of people, whose files were published by Czech's government here: https://archivkgb.zcu.cz/

One file, one NKWD case against a citizen of Czech Republic, one history, where I try to never contradict to the case materials, but I also add some of my hypothesizes when there's an obvious logical gap in the materials. 

As long as this collection is open source and free, you can add your stories (as pull requests), point some logical or any other mistakes (opening issues). Or may be you're some relative or archivist and you know about these people something that doesn't present in file? Feel free to add your bit!
